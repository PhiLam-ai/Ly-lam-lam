# V388 — Face, Neck and Shoulder Refinement Plan

Status: EXPERIMENTAL / NOT APPROVED
Base: `200d29fdb14132f5a8376c3c5cd40988753557d7`
Branch: `avatar/v388-face-neck-refinement`

## Purpose

Improve the isolated procedural avatar candidate without touching the production avatar. V387's successful Blender workflow only proves the candidate can be generated and exported; it does not establish anatomy quality, rigging, or expression quality.

## Problems confirmed in the current builder

- Head, neck, ears, hair, eyes, nose and mouth are separate primitives. A tapered head sphere alone cannot deliver a convincing face.
- The neck is separate from the torso and does not have a connected skin surface.
- Body meshes are generated by voxel fusion, but no humanoid armature or facial shape keys are transferred to the candidate.
- The current render workflow is a technical preview; it is not an approval gate for visual quality.

## V388 work sequence

1. Keep the V387 candidate and its reports as a reproducible baseline.
2. Make the face feature layout parameterized: eye spacing/height, brow height, nose projection, mouth height/width, jaw taper and chin projection. Keep the front-facing axis at -Y.
3. Add a neutral front, left/right three-quarter, and side render set with a close-up crop around the head. Record all image paths in the report.
4. Refine neck-to-shoulder silhouette separately from the head; do not voxel-fuse facial feature meshes into the head because they must remain independently editable.
5. Add geometry checks for missing/degenerate meshes and verify that feature bounds intersect the head's facial region instead of merely passing the export test.
6. Do not add rig/morph transfer until the static face and body silhouette have been visually reviewed. Then test shoulders, elbows, neck/head, jaw and blink keys with before/after renders.
7. Keep production unchanged until visual review, geometry validation, pose deformation and mobile/web viewer checks all pass.

## Acceptance gates

- [ ] Front and three-quarter views show coherent facial proportions.
- [ ] Side view has plausible forehead, nose, lips and chin profile.
- [ ] Neck transitions into shoulders without a visible floating seam.
- [ ] No visible disconnected limbs, holes, or intersecting feature primitives in the render set.
- [ ] Exported GLB opens in the web viewer and renders on mobile.
- [ ] Rig and shape-key deformation pass pose tests and visual review.
- [ ] Human visual review explicitly approves the candidate.

## Non-goals

- This plan does not claim photorealism.
- It does not declare V388 complete.
- It does not authorize merging into `main` or replacing the production GLB.
